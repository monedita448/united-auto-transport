Black Friday to Year-End: How Dealers Keep Inventory Moving

Leer en español

Black Friday through December is the busiest stretch for dealers. How to keep inventory, trades and sold units moving with a reliable transport partner.

Get a free quote Call now

For most dealerships, the stretch from Black Friday to December 31 decides the year. Year-end incentives, holiday shoppers and sales targets all land in the same six weeks. The catch: dealers aren't the only ones who need trucks. Snowbird season and holiday moves fill carrier capacity at the same time. A clear transport plan keeps units moving when every day on the lot counts.

Why Black Friday to year-end strains transport

Three things happen at once:

  • More sold units leave the state. Online and out-of-state buyers want home delivery before the holidays.
  • More trades and transfers. Dealers swap units to match what buyers are asking for.
  • Fewer open spots on trucks. Carriers on the busiest lanes are also loading snowbird cars.

Sold units to out-of-state buyers

An out-of-state buyer who has to wait weeks for delivery may walk away. Door-to-door delivery with a clear pickup window helps close the deal. See how dealer home delivery works.

Dealer trades and transfers

Dealer-to-dealer moves are often short notice. Having a transport partner who already knows your locations, contacts and paperwork saves hours on every trade.

Auction pickups in December

Units bought at auction lose value every day they sit. Plan pickups the same week you buy, and make sure the gate pass and release are ready before the carrier arrives.

Planning a weekly transport schedule

  1. List what has to move this week: sold units, trades, auction buys.
  2. Group by lane so one truck can carry several units.
  3. Book a few days ahead on the busiest lanes; keep expedited options for the urgent ones.
  4. Check every unit on the Bill of Lading at pickup and delivery.

What to expect from a transport partner

  • FMCSA-verified carriers with active insurance
  • One contact who books, tracks and updates you
  • Open transport for everyday units and enclosed for high-value inventory
  • Clear documentation on every move
