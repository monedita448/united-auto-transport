New York to Arizona Car Shipping

Shipping a car from New York to Arizona for the winter? Typical transit time, open vs. enclosed, door-to-door pickup and how to book early. Free quote.

Every winter, more New Yorkers trade snow shovels for desert sun in Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa and Tucson. The catch is the car: New York to Arizona is roughly 2,300 to 2,500 miles, four or five long days behind the wheel through winter weather. Shipping your car door-to-door lets you fly out and find it waiting when you land.

Why New Yorkers winter in Arizona

  • Dry, mild winters. Sunny days in the 60s and 70s instead of slush and ice.
  • Golf, hiking and active communities. The Phoenix area and Tucson are built for snowbird season.
  • A long, hard drive. Crossing Pennsylvania, the Midwest and the plains in December means snow, ice and short days.
  • Your own car all season. Shipping beats months of rental fees and keeps highway miles off the car you'll drive all winter.

Typical transit time

These are typical ranges after pickup, not guarantees:

Route Typical transit after pickup
New York City to Phoenix / Scottsdale about 6 to 9 days
Long Island to Phoenix area about 6 to 9 days
Upstate New York to Tucson about 7 to 10 days

The full timeline also includes the pickup window, the time between booking and the day a carrier picks up your car. November through January is busy on snowbird lanes, so booking two to three weeks ahead gives you more options. See our Arizona snowbird guide for the whole season.

Door-to-door pickup in NYC, Long Island and Upstate

  • New York City: large carriers can't always reach narrow streets or apartment buildings in Manhattan, Brooklyn or Queens. The driver will arrange a nearby open spot, such as a large store parking lot.
  • Long Island and Westchester: pickups at homes in Nassau, Suffolk and Westchester are routine.
  • Upstate New York: Albany, Syracuse, Rochester and Buffalo are covered too. Allow a slightly wider pickup window for rural addresses.

Heading to Florida instead? See our New York to Florida guide.

Open vs. enclosed

Open transport is the most common and affordable choice and works well for daily drivers. In winter, open trailers leaving New York are exposed to road salt and slush, so plan a wash after delivery. Enclosed transport keeps your car fully covered, which makes sense for classic, luxury, low-clearance or high-value vehicles. Learn more about open auto transport.

How to prepare

  1. Wash the car so existing marks are easy to see at inspection.
  2. Take dated photos from every side.
  3. Remove personal items, toll tags (like E-ZPass) and garage remotes.
  4. Leave about a quarter tank of fuel.
  5. Check the battery and tires before cold-weather pickup.
  6. Inspect the car with the driver and sign the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and again at delivery.

How to book

  1. Request a quote with your vehicle, pickup and delivery addresses and preferred dates.
  2. Give a pickup window of a few days. Flexibility gets you a carrier sooner.
  3. Plan around Thanksgiving and the December holidays, when trucks fill fast.
  4. Ask about the return trip. If you're heading back to New York in spring, book that leg early too.
