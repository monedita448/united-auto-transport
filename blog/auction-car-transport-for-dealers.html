Auction Car Transport: Getting Units from Manheim, ADESA and Copart to Your Lot Fast

Bought at auction? We pick up from Manheim, ADESA, Copart and IAA and deliver to your lot. Gate passes, inoperable units, tight storage deadlines handled.

Get a free quote Call now

Winning the bid is only half the job. Until the car is on your lot, it isn't making you money, and once the auction's free storage window closes, it starts costing you.

Here's how to get auction purchases picked up quickly and delivered without surprises.

Why auction pickups stall

Most delays come from the same few issues:

  • Missing or late gate passes and release documents
  • No carrier available for a one-off unit in a less busy location
  • Undisclosed condition (the car doesn't run, has no keys or has flat tires) so the wrong truck shows up
  • Storage deadlines that pass while you're still looking for a carrier

A good transport partner solves all four before they turn into fees.

Auctions we pick up from

We pick up from major physical and online auctions nationwide, including:

  • Manheim
  • ADESA
  • Copart
  • IAA (Insurance Auto Auctions)
  • Dealer-only online auction platforms

Send us the lot location and release paperwork, and we'll handle dispatch.

Running vs. inoperable units

Salvage and insurance auction units often don't run. That's fine, as long as we know in advance:

  • Running units load normally on any carrier.
  • Inoperable units need a winch-equipped carrier.
  • No keys, flat tires or locked steering? Tell us. It changes how the unit is loaded.

Accurate condition details are the fastest way to avoid a failed pickup. See our specialized services.

What the carrier needs from you

  • Year, make, model and VIN
  • Auction location and lot or stock number
  • Gate pass or release documentation
  • Running condition and key status
  • Delivery address and a contact at your store

Inspection and BOL at pickup

At pickup, the driver inspects the unit and records its condition on the Bill of Lading (BOL). At delivery, your team checks the unit against that record before signing. For auction cars that already have damage, this step matters even more: it separates what was there before from anything that happened in transit.

Recurring auction lanes for dealers

If you buy from the same auctions every week, a recurring plan saves time:

  • Standing pickup days at your regular auctions
  • Your locations, contacts and paperwork already on file
  • Faster dispatch and more predictable delivery

This is part of our broader dealer auto transport service.
