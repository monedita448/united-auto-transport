Shipping a Car from California to New York: Timeline, Options and Tips

How long does it take to ship a car from California to New York? Typical 7–10 day transit, open vs. enclosed options and booking tips. Get your free quote.

Get a free quote Call now

Moving from the West Coast to the East Coast is a big step. Driving about 2,800 miles across the country on top of everything else is a lot to ask. Shipping your car coast to coast lets you focus on the move itself.

Here's what to expect when you ship a car from California to New York.

Coast to coast at a glance

  • Distance: about 2,800 miles
  • Typical transit: about 7 to 10 days door-to-door after pickup
  • Carrier types: open or enclosed
  • Service: door-to-door pickup and delivery

For route specifics, see our California to New York car shipping page.

Pickup cities in California

We pick up across the state, including:

  • Los Angeles and Orange County
  • San Diego
  • The Bay Area (San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose)
  • Sacramento

Major metros are on busy carrier routes, which usually means faster dispatch. Rural pickups are absolutely possible but may need a slightly wider window.

Delivery in New York City vs. Upstate

New York City: Large carriers often can't drive down narrow or restricted streets. Your driver may arrange to meet you at a nearby accessible location. We'll coordinate this ahead of time.

Upstate New York (Buffalo, Rochester, Albany, Syracuse): Access is usually easier, but winter weather can affect timing from November onward.

Weather and mountain passes

A cross-country route passes through very different climates, from desert heat to mountain passes and Northeast winter weather. Professional carriers plan around this, but in winter a few extra days of flexibility help.

When enclosed transport makes sense

Most coast-to-coast moves use open transport. Consider enclosed if your car is:

  • A classic, collector or exotic vehicle
  • Low-clearance and needs liftgate loading
  • New, freshly detailed or high-value, and you want full protection over 2,800 miles
