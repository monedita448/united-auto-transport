Bought a Car on Black Friday? How to Ship It Home

Leer en español

Bought a car online or out of state on Black Friday? How to ship it home door-to-door, what to ask the dealer, the documents you need and how to inspect it on the BOL.

Get a free quote Call now

Black Friday is one of the biggest weekends of the year for car buying, and more of those deals are out of state or online. If the car you just bought is hundreds or thousands of miles away, you don't have to fly out and drive it home. A carrier can pick it up at the dealer and deliver it to your door.

Why Black Friday buyers ship instead of drive

  • No one-way flight and long drive. Shipping saves travel days, hotels and fuel.
  • No extra miles on a new car. The odometer stays close to where it was when you bought it.
  • Holiday timing. Late November roads are busy and winter weather is starting on northern routes.
  • Convenience. You keep working and spending the holidays at home while the car is on the way.

Coordinate pickup with the selling dealer

Before you book, ask the dealer a few questions:

  • Who will release the car, and what hours can a driver pick up?
  • Is the car paid off and ready to go, with any dealer prep finished?
  • Where should the truck load? Large carriers often load in the lot or on a nearby street.
  • Can they hold the car for a few days while a carrier is assigned?

Many dealers ship cars to out-of-state buyers all the time. Our dealer home delivery guide explains how it works from the dealer's side.

Documents and keys

  • Your purchase paperwork, so the dealer can release the car to the carrier
  • The name and phone number of the dealer contact at pickup
  • At least one working key handed to the driver
  • Registration and temp tags as the dealer and your state require

The dealer handles title and registration paperwork according to the sale. The carrier just needs to know the car is released and where it's going.

Open vs. enclosed for a new car

Open transport is the most common and affordable option and works for most new and used cars. In winter, open trailers are exposed to road salt and spray, so plan a wash after delivery. Enclosed transport keeps the car fully covered, which many buyers choose for luxury, exotic or high-value cars. Compare both in our open vs. enclosed guide.

Inspecting at delivery on the Bill of Lading

At pickup, the driver and the dealer inspect the car together and record its condition on the Bill of Lading (BOL). At delivery:

  1. Inspect the car in good light, all the way around.
  2. Compare it against the pickup BOL and any photos from the dealer.
  3. Note any new damage on the BOL before signing, and take photos.
  4. Keep your copy.

Learn more in our Bill of Lading guide.

How to book

  1. Request a quote with the dealer's address, your delivery address and the vehicle.
  2. Confirm the dealer contact and pickup hours.
  3. Give a pickup window of a few days, especially over the holiday weekend.
  4. Be ready at delivery to inspect and sign the BOL.
