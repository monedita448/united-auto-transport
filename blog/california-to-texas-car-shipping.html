California to Texas Car Shipping: What to Expect When You Move to the Lone Star State

Moving from California to Texas? Typical transit to Dallas, Austin and Houston, open vs. enclosed options and how to book door-to-door. Free quote.

Get a free quote Call now

New job, lower cost of living, family nearby: whatever the reason, you're heading to Texas. The drive is long and mostly desert. Shipping your car door-to-door lets you fly in, get settled and have your car show up ready to go.

Why CA→TX is one of the busiest relocation lanes

California to Texas is a well-traveled relocation route, and it moves both ways. That two-way traffic is good news for you: carriers running between the two states want full loads in each direction, which helps keep dispatch moving.

Heading the other way? See our Texas to California car shipping guide.

Transit expectations

  • Distance: about 1,200 to 1,900 miles, depending on your California origin and Texas destination
  • Typical transit: about 4 to 7 days after pickup
  • Service: door-to-door, open or enclosed

Southern California to Dallas or Houston is usually on the faster end. Bay Area and Sacramento pickups add distance and a few extra days.

Pickup metros

  • Los Angeles and Orange County: the most common origin, close to I-10 and I-40
  • San Diego and the Inland Empire: frequent pickups on the main eastbound corridors
  • Bay Area and Sacramento: regular pickups, with a slightly longer trip
  • Central Valley: Fresno and Bakersfield pickups are common too

In dense neighborhoods, the driver may meet you at a nearby accessible spot if a large carrier can't reach your street.

Delivery metros

  • Dallas–Fort Worth: a major carrier hub, so it's a popular way to ship a car from LA to Dallas
  • Austin: California to Austin car shipping is a frequent request for tech relocations
  • Houston: busy and well served by carriers
  • San Antonio and El Paso: El Paso is the first major Texas stop, which can shorten transit

Registering your car in Texas (overview)

Once you arrive, you'll need to switch your registration from California to Texas. In general:

  • Get proof of Texas auto insurance
  • Check whether your county requires a vehicle inspection
  • Register and title the car through your county tax assessor-collector's office

New residents have a limited window to register. Check the Texas Department of Motor Vehicles (TxDMV) for current deadlines and requirements. You don't need Texas registration before shipping: ship the car first and handle it after you arrive.

How it works
