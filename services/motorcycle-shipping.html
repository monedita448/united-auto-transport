Motorcycle Shipping, Door to Door

Moving a motorcycle across the country, buying one out of state, or sending your bike south for the season? We ship motorcycles door to door on enclosed carriers, so your bike travels protected from the weather and road debris instead of exposed on an open trailer.


Call now or leave your number, tell your specialist the make and model, your pickup and delivery locations and your dates, and we'll match your bike with a vetted, FMCSA-verified carrier and give you a real, current price.

How Much Does It Cost to Ship a Motorcycle?

Estimated prices for a motorcycle in good running condition, shipped door to door on an enclosed carrier:

DistanceMotorcycle (enclosed carrier)
About 1,000 miles$1,100 – $1,500
About 1,500 miles$1,550 – $2,150
About 2,000 miles$2,000 – $2,800

Shorter trips cost more per mile. Prices are always changing with demand: snowbird season, spring and summer moving season can all push rates up on busy routes. Get Your Motorcycle Ready for Pickup

  • Take clear photos of your bike from every side before pickup.
  • Note any existing scratches or damage so they match the inspection report.
  • Remove or secure loose accessories such as bags, GPS units and phone mounts.
  • Ask your specialist about fuel level and battery requirements, since they can vary by carrier.
  • Have the keys ready, and tell us if you don't have them.
