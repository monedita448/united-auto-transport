What Is Expedited Car Shipping?

Expedited shipping gives your car priority dispatching, compressed pickup windows and dedicated tracking, so it moves sooner than a standard order. Because your car takes priority, expedited always costs more than standard shipping.


It works on the same services you already know, including open and enclosed transport, with door-to-door pickup and delivery.

When Expedited Makes Sense

  • A buyer has signed and is waiting on the car.
  • A month-end or manufacturer program deadline is coming up.
  • An auction storage deadline is about to pass.
  • You have a firm move date you can't miss.

For routine moves with flexible dates, standard shipping is usually the better value. Shipping for a dealership? See dealer auto transport.

How Much Does Expedited Shipping Cost?

Expedited always costs more than standard. By how much depends on the route, the vehicle, how soon you need it and what the market is doing, so we give you an exact price on the phone instead of a generic number. The typical prices in our car shipping cost guide are for standard shipping.
