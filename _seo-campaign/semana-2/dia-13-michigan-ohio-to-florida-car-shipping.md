# Michigan and Ohio to Florida Car Shipping: A Midwest Snowbird Guide

When the lake-effect snow starts, Michigan and Ohio snowbirds head for Florida. Instead of spending two days on I-75, more of them fly down and have their car shipped door-to-door.

## Midwest to Florida at a glance

- **Distance:** roughly 1,000 to 1,400 miles, depending on origin and destination
- **Typical transit:** about 3 to 6 days after pickup
- **Main corridor:** I-75, one of the busiest north–south carrier routes

## Popular pickup areas

- **Michigan:** Metro Detroit, Grand Rapids, Lansing, Ann Arbor, Traverse City
- **Ohio:** Cleveland, Columbus, Cincinnati, Toledo, Dayton

Cities along I-75 often see the fastest dispatch. Northern Michigan pickups are possible but may need a wider window, especially once the snow starts.

## Picking up in snow season

- **Book before the first big storm** when you can. Early November is calmer than December.
- **Clear the driveway or plan a nearby meeting spot.** Carriers can't navigate icy residential hills.
- **Keep your dates flexible.** Weather can shift pickup by a day or two.

## Favorite Florida destinations

Midwest snowbirds tend to head to:

- Fort Myers, Naples and Cape Coral
- Sarasota, Venice and Bradenton
- Tampa, Clearwater and St. Petersburg
- The Villages and Central Florida

## FAQ

**My car has winter tires. Is that a problem?**
No. Just make sure the tires are properly inflated.

**Should I wash the salt off before pickup?**
Yes. A clean car makes the pickup inspection accurate.

**Can you pick up from my storage unit?**
Usually yes, as long as a large carrier can access it or the car can be brought out to the street.
