# Massachusetts to Florida Car Shipping: Boston Snowbirds' Guide

New England winters are beautiful from a distance. Every fall, Massachusetts snowbirds head to Florida, and more of them are choosing to fly down and ship the car instead of spending two or three days on I-95.

## Route overview

- **Distance:** about 1,250 to 1,500 miles, depending on your Florida destination
- **Typical transit:** about 4 to 6 days after pickup
- **Service:** door-to-door, open or enclosed

Southbound demand peaks from **October through January**, so the earlier you book, the more pickup dates you'll have to choose from.

## Pickup tips for Boston and the rest of Massachusetts

- **Boston, Cambridge and Somerville:** narrow streets and parking restrictions are common. If a full-size carrier can't reach your door, the driver will arrange to meet you at a nearby accessible spot.
- **Suburbs and the South Shore:** usually easy access.
- **Cape Cod and the Islands:** Cape pickups are possible with a bit more planning. For island vehicles, plan to bring the car to the mainland.
- **Central and Western Mass (Worcester, Springfield):** close to major carrier corridors.

## Popular Florida destinations

- Naples, Fort Myers and Sarasota
- Palm Beach, Boca Raton and Delray Beach
- Orlando and The Villages
- Tampa and St. Petersburg

## When to book

- **2 to 3 weeks ahead** is ideal
- **Flexible dates** make dispatch faster
- **Plan your spring return** at the same time

## Open or enclosed?

Open transport is the most common and economical choice. Many New England snowbirds who spend the winter driving a classic or convertible in Florida choose [enclosed auto transport](https://www.united-autotransport.com/services/enclosed-auto-transport.html) for extra protection.

## FAQ

**Can you pick up from my condo or a parking garage?**
Usually the driver meets you at the curb or a nearby open area, because carriers can't enter garages. We'll coordinate the details.

**Can I leave my E-ZPass in the car?**
Remove it, or the car could rack up tolls on the carrier's route.

**Do you ship Florida back to Massachusetts in spring?**
Yes. Book both legs together if you know your dates.
