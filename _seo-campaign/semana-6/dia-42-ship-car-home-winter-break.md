# Shipping a Car Home for Winter Break

Finals are almost over and winter break is close. For college students who drove to a campus far from home, that raises a question: what happens to the car? Leaving it on campus for a month isn't always allowed or safe, and a long solo drive home in December weather is a lot to ask after finals week. Shipping the car door-to-door is one way to get it home while you fly.

## Why students ship instead of drive home

- **Long distances.** Many students attend school hundreds or thousands of miles from home. That's one or more days of solo driving.
- **Winter weather.** December roads across the Midwest, the Northeast and mountain passes can be icy and unpredictable.
- **Finals fatigue.** A long drive right after exams isn't the safest plan.
- **Campus rules.** Some schools limit where cars can stay over break, and some students would rather not leave a car parked for weeks.

## When to book around finals and holidays

Winter break overlaps with the busiest weeks of the year for carrier capacity, when snowbirds, holiday travelers and dealers are all booking trucks. A few tips:

- **Book two to three weeks ahead,** ideally before finals start.
- **Give a pickup window** of a few days, timed after your last exam.
- **Avoid the holidays themselves.** Carriers keep running, but pickups and deliveries on Christmas Day are less common.
- **Plan delivery at home** for a day when a parent or another trusted adult can meet the driver.

## Pickup on or near campus

Large carriers can't always reach dorms, narrow campus roads or parking garages. Common solutions:

- **A nearby open lot,** such as a large store parking lot close to campus
- **An off-campus apartment** with street access
- **A friend or roommate** who stays nearby and can hand over the car if you leave before pickup

Whoever hands over the car inspects it with the driver and signs the Bill of Lading (BOL). At home, a parent or trusted adult can inspect and sign at delivery.

## Leave it home or ship it back in January

Think about the spring semester before you book:

- **Ship home and back.** If you need the car on campus in spring, plan the return trip now so it arrives before classes start.
- **Leave it home.** If you don't need a car next semester, shipping it home once may be all you need.
- **Ship one way and fly back.** Some families ship the car home for break and decide on the return in January.

## How to prepare the car

1. Remove personal items, dorm stuff and parking permits.
2. Leave about a quarter tank of fuel.
3. Check the battery and tire pressure, which cold weather affects.
4. Wash the car and take dated photos from every side.
5. Make sure the driver gets a working key.

## FAQ

**Can a parent book the shipment for a student?**
Yes. Parents often book and pay, while the student or a friend hands over the car on campus.

**Can you pick up from a dorm?**
If the truck can't reach the dorm, the driver will arrange a nearby meeting spot, such as a large parking lot.

**How long does it take to ship a car home?**
It depends on the distance and the route. Shorter regional moves can take a few days after pickup, and coast-to-coast moves take longer. Your quote includes an estimated range.
