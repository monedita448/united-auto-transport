# Holiday Car Shipping 2026: When to Book for Thanksgiving and Christmas

If you want your car waiting for you at the holidays, the most important decision is when you book. From mid-November through New Year's, three groups compete for the same carrier trucks: snowbirds heading south, dealers moving year-end inventory, and families relocating or sending a car to someone for the holidays. Booking early is the simplest way to get the pickup window you want.

## Why holiday weeks fill carrier trucks

A carrier truck has a fixed number of spots, usually seven to ten cars on an open trailer and fewer on an enclosed one. In late fall, the busiest lanes (Northeast and Midwest to Florida and Arizona) fill quickly. Holiday weeks also have fewer working days, so the same demand is squeezed into less time.

What that means for you: the closer you book to the holiday, the fewer pickup options you have.

## When to book for Thanksgiving delivery

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26, 2026. If you need your car delivered before the holiday:

- **Book as soon as you can.** If you're reading this the week before, call us and we'll tell you honestly what is still possible on your route.
- **Be flexible on pickup.** A pickup window of a few days gives us more trucks to choose from.
- **Expect a quieter Thursday.** Many carriers keep driving during Thanksgiving week, but pickups and deliveries on the holiday itself are less common.

## When to book for Christmas delivery

For delivery before Christmas Day (Friday, December 25), we recommend booking **two to three weeks ahead**, and earlier for coast-to-coast routes. That leaves room for the pickup window plus transit time, which on long routes can be a week or more.

Booking in early December also gives you a buffer if winter weather slows a truck down.

## What changes during holiday weeks

- **Pickup windows get tighter.** Carriers plan their loads days in advance. A late booking may only fit a truck that is already partly full on your lane.
- **Someone must be there.** At pickup and at delivery, someone needs to inspect the car with the driver and sign the Bill of Lading (BOL). Over the holidays, line up a backup person in case you're traveling.
- **Weather can shift dates.** Snow in the Midwest and Northeast can delay a truck by a day or two. Good carriers keep you updated.

## Open vs. enclosed in winter

Open transport is the standard choice and works well for most cars, even in winter. If your car is new, high-value or classic, enclosed transport protects it from road salt, snow and debris. ## FAQ

**How far in advance should I book car shipping for Christmas?**
Two to three weeks ahead is a good rule, and earlier for coast-to-coast routes. Booking early gives you more pickup options and a buffer for winter weather.

**Do car carriers work on Thanksgiving and Christmas?**
Many carriers keep driving during holiday weeks, but pickups and deliveries on the holiday itself are less common. Plan your dates around the holiday, not on it.

**Can I still ship my car if the holiday is next week?**
