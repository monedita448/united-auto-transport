# North Carolina to Florida Car Shipping

Every fall, thousands of North Carolina drivers head to Florida for the winter, to see family over the holidays or to start a new job. The drive down I-95 or I-77 takes a full day or more, plus gas, hotels and miles on your car. Shipping your car door-to-door lets you fly or ride down and find your car waiting for you.

## Why NC drivers ship to Florida in winter

- **Snowbird season.** Retirees from Charlotte, Raleigh, Asheville and the coast spend the winter in Florida.
- **Holiday travel.** Families fly to Florida for Thanksgiving and Christmas and want their own car there.
- **Relocation.** New jobs and military moves between the two states are common.

## Typical transit time

North Carolina to Florida is a mid-length route, so transit is usually a few days once the car is picked up. The full timeline also includes the pickup window, which depends on how busy the lane is. In November and December, the Southeast to Florida lanes are busy, so booking early gives you more pickup options.

## Door-to-door pickup across North Carolina

We pick up from Charlotte, Raleigh-Durham, Greensboro, Winston-Salem, Wilmington, Asheville and smaller towns across the state. If your street is hard for a large truck to reach, the driver will meet you at a nearby open lot.

## Open vs. enclosed

Open transport is the standard choice and works well for most daily drivers. For a new, high-value or classic car, enclosed transport keeps it away from road debris and weather. [Compare open and enclosed transport](https://www.united-autotransport.com/blog/open-vs-enclosed-car-transport).

## How to prepare

1. Wash the car so existing marks are easy to see at inspection.
2. Take photos from every side.
3. Remove personal items and toll tags.
4. Leave about a quarter tank of fuel.
5. Inspect the car with the driver and sign the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and delivery.

## How to book before the holidays
