# Popular Car Shipping Routes in the U.S. and Typical Transit Times

"How long does car shipping take?" is one of the first questions people ask. The honest answer depends on the route, the season and how flexible your dates are. This guide collects typical transit times for the most popular routes we cover, with a link to the full guide for each one.

## How transit time is estimated

Car shipping has two parts:

1. **Pickup window:** the time between booking and the day a carrier picks up your car. It's usually a few days, and it depends on how busy the lane is.
2. **Transit:** the time from pickup to delivery. It depends mainly on distance, because carrier drivers follow federal hours-of-service rules and usually cover a few hundred miles a day.

The times below are **transit after pickup**, door-to-door, for open or enclosed transport.

## What speeds it up or slows it down

**Faster:**
- Pickup and delivery near major highways and metro areas
- Flexible pickup dates, so the first available carrier can take your car
- Busy, well-traveled lanes with lots of carrier traffic
- Expedited service, which shortens the pickup window

**Slower:**
- Rural or hard-to-reach addresses, which add a stop or a meeting point
- Winter weather on northern routes and mountain passes
- Holidays, when fewer drivers are on the road
- Enclosed transport on less common lanes, since there are fewer enclosed carriers
- Inoperable vehicles that need a winch-equipped carrier

## Snowbird season effect

From about October through January, southbound trucks to Florida and Arizona fill up fast, and from March through May the flow reverses. During those months, pickup windows on snowbird lanes can stretch. Book 2 to 3 weeks ahead, give a pickup window of a few days and plan around Thanksgiving and the December holidays. Our [snowbird car shipping guide](https://www.united-autotransport.com/blog/snowbird-car-shipping-guide) covers the full season.

Whatever the route, every shipment moves with an FMCSA-verified carrier, and your car's condition is recorded on the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and delivery.

## FAQ

**Can you guarantee a delivery date?**
No honest broker can guarantee an exact delivery hour on a long route, because weather and traffic matter. We give you a pickup window and an estimated delivery range, and keep you updated.

**Does open or enclosed transport change the transit time?**
Usually not much once the car is picked up. Enclosed can take a bit longer to schedule on less common lanes.
