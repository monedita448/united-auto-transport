# California to Florida Car Shipping: Coast-to-Coast Guide

Driving from California to Florida means crossing the entire southern half of the country: deserts, Texas, the Gulf Coast and the long run down the Florida peninsula. That's close to a week behind the wheel, plus hotels, meals and wear on your car. California to Florida car shipping lets you skip the trip and have your car picked up and delivered door-to-door while you fly or handle the rest of your move.

## Route overview (~2,500–2,800 mi)

- **Distance:** roughly 2,500 to 2,800 miles, depending on your exact pickup and delivery cities
- **Typical transit:** about 7 to 10 days after pickup
- **Service:** door-to-door, open or enclosed

Most carriers run this lane along the southern interstates, primarily I-10, from Southern California through Arizona, New Mexico and Texas, then across Louisiana, Mississippi and Alabama into Florida. Northern California pickups usually head south first to join the corridor. It's a well-traveled coast-to-coast lane, much like our [California to New York car shipping guide](https://www.united-autotransport.com/blog/california-to-new-york-car-shipping-guide) route, with steady carrier traffic in both directions.

## Typical transit

Most cross country car shipping to Florida from California is completed in about a week to a week and a half once the car is on the truck. A few things can shift that range:

- **Pickup window:** carriers fill their trailers along the route, so it can take a few days to assign your car to a truck
- **Distance within each state:** Northern California to South Florida is the longest version of this trip
- **Season:** fall and winter snowbird demand can make Florida-bound lanes busier
- **Weather:** Gulf Coast storms during hurricane season (June through November) can cause short delays

We'll give you an estimated window when you book and keep you updated if anything changes.

## Pickup & delivery metros

**Common California pickups:**

- Los Angeles and Orange County
- San Diego and the Inland Empire
- San Francisco Bay Area and Sacramento

**Common Florida deliveries:**

- Miami, Fort Lauderdale and West Palm Beach
- Orlando and Tampa
- Jacksonville and Naples

If you need to ship a car from LA to Miami, you're on one of the most requested versions of this route. Rural addresses are fine too; just allow a slightly wider pickup or delivery window. If a full-size carrier can't safely reach your street, the driver will arrange to meet you at a nearby accessible spot, like a large parking lot.

## Open vs. enclosed

**Open transport** is the most common and economical choice. Your car rides on the same type of carrier dealerships use to move new inventory, and open trucks are widely available on this lane.

**[Enclosed auto transport](https://www.united-autotransport.com/services/enclosed-auto-transport.html)** keeps your car fully covered from road debris, weather and dust. It's the better fit for classic, exotic, luxury or low-clearance cars, or any vehicle you simply want fully protected for a coast-to-coast trip.

Either way, your car's condition is recorded on the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and checked again at delivery.

## FAQ

**Can I ship my car from Florida back to California?**
Yes. We ship both directions on this route.

**Can I leave personal items in the car?**
Carriers generally ask that the car be empty of personal belongings. Ask us about your specific situation when you book.

**Do I need to be there for pickup and delivery?**
You or a trusted adult you designate needs to be present to inspect the car and sign the BOL.
