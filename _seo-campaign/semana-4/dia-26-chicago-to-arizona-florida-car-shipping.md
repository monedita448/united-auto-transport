# Chicago to Arizona and Florida Car Shipping: Illinois Snowbirds' Guide

When the first hard freeze hits Chicago, many Illinois residents start counting the days until they head somewhere warm. Most choose one of two destinations: the Arizona desert or the Florida coast. Either way, the drive is long, and it's not much fun in November weather. Shipping your car door-to-door lets you fly down, settle in and have your own car waiting for the season.

## Two favorite winter destinations

**Arizona.** Phoenix, Scottsdale, Mesa, Sun City and Tucson draw Midwest snowbirds who want dry heat, golf and mountain views. Illinois to Arizona car shipping typically runs west along I-80 or I-44 and I-40 across the Plains.

**Florida.** Orlando, Tampa, Sarasota, Fort Myers, Naples and the Atlantic coast are long-time favorites. Chicago to Florida car shipping usually runs south through Indiana, Kentucky, Tennessee and Georgia on I-65 and I-75.

Both are well-traveled snowbird lanes. ## Chicago pickup tips

Chicago is a major carrier hub, which helps. A few local tips make pickup smoother:

- **City streets and high-rises:** Full-size carriers can't always reach narrow streets, alleys or downtown buildings. The driver may meet you at a nearby accessible spot, like a large store parking lot.
- **Suburbs:** Pickups in Naperville, Schaumburg, Oak Brook and the North Shore are common and usually straightforward.
- **Winter weather:** Snow and ice can affect pickup timing. Keep your driveway clear and your phone on.
- **Downstate and rural Illinois:** Fine to ship from, but allow a slightly wider pickup window.

Before pickup, wash the car so existing marks are easy to see, leave about a quarter tank of fuel and remove personal items. Your car's condition is recorded on the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and checked again at delivery.

## Booking tips

- **Book early.** Fall is the busiest time of year on south- and west-bound snowbird lanes. Booking a few weeks ahead gives you more carrier options.
- **Stay flexible.** A pickup window of a few days often makes for a smoother match.
- **Plan your return.** If you'll head back to Illinois in spring, ask about booking the return trip early too, since spring is the reverse peak.
- **Choose your transport type.** Open transport is the most common and economical choice. Enclosed is worth considering for classic, luxury or low-clearance cars.
