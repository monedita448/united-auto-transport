How Fast Can We Pick Up?

Pickup is usually within 24 to 48 hours. The exact timing depends on three things: the route, the type of vehicle, and how many vehicles you're moving. A single running sedan on a busy lane moves faster than a multi-car order, an enclosed unit or a vehicle from a remote location.


If a deadline is tight (a signed customer waiting on the car, month-end, an auction storage cutoff), tell us when you call and ask about expedited dispatch. The sooner we know a unit is released, the sooner we can plan the truck.

Bring Volume, Get a Deal

There are always deals for dealers as long as you bring quantity: the more units you move, the better the deal. Call with your typical monthly volume and your most common origins and destinations, and we'll build a quote and a plan around them.


For recurring lanes (the same auction every week, the same rooftops, the same markets), we keep your locations and contacts on file so dispatch is faster and every shipment follows one standard process.

Verified Carriers and Clean Documentation

United Auto Transport is a licensed property broker. We work only with independently licensed and insured carriers, and before a carrier takes your unit we:

  • Check DOT and MC authority directly with the FMCSA
  • Verify the carrier's insurance before pickup
  • Require a documented inspection on the Bill of Lading (BOL) at pickup and delivery

If a claim is ever needed, the BOL record is what protects you. Read about carrier liability.

Non-Running Units

Salvage, insurance and project units often don't run. That's fine, as long as we know in advance. Non-running vehicles ship on winch-equipped carriers, and winch loading typically adds about $125 to the regular rate. If a unit has no wheels or can't roll, it must be loaded and unloaded with a forklift, and you are responsible for arranging one. See inoperable vehicle shipping for details.
